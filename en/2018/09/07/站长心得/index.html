Webmaster's Notes

wlb
2018-09-07
建站, 经验

Research

    1. Website entrepreneurship carries low risk but still requires caution
    1. Set entrepreneurial goals objectively according to your own situation
    1. As a webmaster, you must have your own judgment
    1. A webmaster needs individuality
    1. Success also has its limits; know when to stop and you will find endless joy

Site Building

    1. Building a website does not require any profound technology
    1. Teach yourself website technology via the Internet
    1. Do not build spam sites or sensitive sites
    1. Do not blindly follow trends, and do not chase completeness and size
    1. A few hundred yuan is enough to build a website
    1. Niche websites have a higher success rate
    1. Multi-syllable pinyin is the first choice
    1. The site name must be easy to remember

Promotion

    1. Spend as little money as possible on website promotion
    1. Do not refuse to do something just because it is small
    1. Offline activities are the best promotion method

Marketing

    1. Email marketing is the preferred online promotion method for individual webmasters
    1. Email marketing needs interaction
    1. Email marketing must avoid disputes
    1. Email content must be original and ingenious

Hype

    1. Hype must not harm the interests of others
    1. Hype requires courage
    1. Hype must cater to the taste of the target audience

Management

    1. A webmaster must be all things to all people
    1. Do not take rankings and traffic too seriously
    1. A website with noise and bustle has vitality
    1. Individual ability is limited; learn to make use of people
    1. Playing the underdog role makes it easier to gain the sympathy and support of netizens
    1. Closer distance does not necessarily mean a better relationship

Profit

    1. The earlier the commercialization, the better
    1. Open up profit channels widely
    1. A website that can make money is a good website

Practice

Task Schedule
Month 1 onward: ICP filing
1. Clearly understand whether you are suited to being a webmaster
2. Decide what website to build and whether there is a clear goal; community and forum websites are recommended
3. Start learning website-building knowledge online
4. Decide the domain name and site name
5. Purchase a domain and hosting space
6. File the domain name at the MIIT filing website
Month 2 onward: launch
1. Install the website
2. A preliminary logo
3. Beautify and optimize the website
Months 3-4 onward: establish a helpful image
1. Begin targeted, precise promotion
2. Enthusiastically answer netizens’ questions
3. Achieve 100 IP visits per day
Months 5-6 onward: email marketing
1. Begin collecting relevant netizens’ email addresses, and launch mass email invitations for netizens to come and give guidance
2. Exchange friend links
3. Register IDs on relevant websites for future posting and promotion
4. Achieve 300 IP visits per day
Months 6-8 onward: publish advertorials
1. Write advertorials and publish them on various websites
2. Collect this site’s member data, such as QQ, email, and mobile numbers, and mass-send useful materials to members
3. Look for partner media, such as magazines, newspapers, and websites, for resource exchange
4. Achieve 500 IP visits per day
Months 8-10 onward: apply for ad alliance banners
1. Continue doing the previous tasks well, and apply for paid ad banners from ad alliances to hang on the website
2. Achieve 1,000 IP visits per day
Months 10-12 onward: find advertisers
1. Consider looking for advertisers
2. Achieve 2,000 IP visits per day
Months 12-15 onward: offline activities
1. The website has gained a certain level of popularity; some offline meetups of netizens can be organized
2. The website can sell some merchandise, such as site souvenirs
3. Begin researching some form of paid service
4. Achieve monthly revenue of 2,000-3,000 yuan and 3,000 IP visits per day
Months 15-18 onward: webmaster packaging
1. The webmaster enters media coverage
2. Monthly revenue around 3,000 yuan and 5,000 IPs per day
Months 18-24 onward: the website is thriving
1. Achieve 10,000 IP visits per day
2. Have a certain influence in the industry
3. The total number of effective mass emails reaches over 100,000
4. Various revenues reach 5,000 yuan per month
Months 24-30: stable income
1. Total revenue from various businesses exceeds 5,000 yuan per month
2. Deepen the previous tasks
3. The website has a good reputation
Months 30-36: incorporate and operate as a company
1. Achieve over 20,000 unique IP visits per day
2. Revenue reaches 10,000 yuan per month
3. Register a small company
4. Complete ICP filing for interactive information services (for the specific procedures, search online or consult the relevant departments)
5. Recruit talent and set up a small team

Process for an Individual Webmaster to Establish a Private Internet Company

  1. Establishing a company requires at least two shareholders; besides the webmaster, you must find a partner or relative to be the other shareholder.
  2. Prepare 100,000 yuan in cash as the corporate registered capital (some places require 30,000 yuan).
  3. Decide the company name, which usually contains “Network Technology” or “Information Services”.
  4. Prepare both shareholders’ ID cards, family planning certificates (or unmarried certificates), and the office property ownership certificate, and obtain the enterprise application form from the Industry and Commerce Administration Bureau.
  5. After submitting the application form, if the conditions are met and the company name has not been registered, it can be approved.
  6. After approval, the industry and commerce personnel will notify you to open the company’s temporary bank account at a bank and deposit the registered capital.
  7. Find an accounting firm to verify the capital, and submit the capital verification certificate to the Industry and Commerce Administration Bureau.
  8. Obtain the business license.
  9. Register for tax at the Local Taxation Bureau.
  10. Complete the organization code at the Quality and Technical Supervision Bureau.
  11. Complete the employment permit at the Human Resources and Social Security Bureau (Labor Bureau).
  12. Complete employee social insurance at the Social Insurance Bureau.
    The above is the process I followed when registering my company; it took about 20~30 days and cost around 1,500 yuan, which may vary from place to place. If the registered capital is insufficient, you can borrow from relatives and friends, and it can be withdrawn after 3 months; the lower the company’s registered capital, the lower the fixed-amount tax.

Staffing of a Private Internet Company (website)

Position Function
Company CEO 1. Fully responsible for the company’s profitability
2. Plan product and website development direction
3. Handle major affairs and cooperation
4. Directly responsible for advertising sales
Sales Manager 1. Responsible for the sales, shipping, processing and production of the company’s products
2. After-sales service
3. Cashier
Programmer and System Maintainer 1. Add or modify a feature
2. Troubleshoot website faults
Designer 1. Web design and animation production
2. Page content updates
Consulting Representative 1. Online customer service for the website
2. Company finance custodian (resident at the company)
Front Desk 1. Front desk reception
2. Website administrator
3. Member hotline
Business Webmaster 1. Promote website products
2. Organize various activities
3. Represent the website in visits to various places
Consultant 1. Provide various suggestions or opinions on website operation
2. Write books about the website for publication
